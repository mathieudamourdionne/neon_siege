HOW TO PLAY
ONE HIT KILLS YOU. ONE HIT KILLS THEM. WASD - MOVE · MOUSE - AIM · LEFT CLICK - ATTACK / SHOOT RIGHT CLICK - PICK UP / THROW WEAPON SPACE - GET ON A DOWNED ENEMY, THEN CLICK TO SMASH SHIFT (HOLD) - LOOK FURTHER · MIDDLE CLICK / CTRL - LOCK ON E - FREE CAPTIVES / USE · F - IMPLANT ABILITY · G - GRENADE R - RESTART FLOOR · ESC - PAUSE FISTS KNOCK PEOPLE DOWN. SWING A DOOR INTO SOMEONE TO FLATTEN THEM. GUNSHOTS ARE LOUD. QUICK KILLS BUILD YOUR COMBO. JOBS: E BAGS LOOT, E AGAIN THROWS THE BAG. BAGS SLOW YOU, THE SAFE STOPS YOU FIGHTING.
SAFEHOUSE: LEVELS AND JOBS PAY CASH. SPEND IT ON A STARTING WEAPON, UPGRADES, JACKETS AND JOB PLANNING. TALL THINGS (LOCKERS, PILLARS, CRATES, RACKS) STOP BULLETS. TABLES DON'T. ENEMIES GUARD (BLUE HELMET) - THE BASIC ONE. PIPES, PISTOLS, SMGS. BREACHER (RED HELMET) - CHARGES YOU WITH A SHOTGUN. CLEANER (BLACK SUIT) - FAST, SIDESTEPS WHILE SHOOTING. JUGGERNAUT (BIG) - PUNCHES DON'T DROP HIM. 3 BULLETS, A SAW OR A DOOR. HOUND - VERY FAST, DIES TO ONE HIT. WATCHER (GREEN VISOR) - SNIPER. MOVE WHEN THE LASER TURNS HOT. SCRAPPER (ORANGE, HARD HAT) - FAST BRAWLER. PIPES ONLY KNOCK PEOPLE DOWN SOMETIMES. DOCK TWINS - BOSSES. YOU'LL NEED THE CRANE.
